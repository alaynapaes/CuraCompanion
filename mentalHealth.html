Mental Health Support for Caregivers

Caring for a loved one can be emotionally exhausting. CuraCompanion provides simple mental-wellness tools to help caregivers manage stress and prevent burnout.

Quick Stress-Relief Tools

Guided Breathing (2 minutes)

4-4-6 Breathing Technique:

  • Inhale for 4 seconds
  • Hold for 4 seconds
  • Exhale for 6 seconds

Grounding Exercise

5-4-3-2-1 Method:

  • 5 things you can see
  • 4 things you can feel
  • 3 things you can hear
  • 2 things you can smell
  • 1 thing you can taste

Frequently Asked Questions

What is caregiver burnout?
Emotional and physical exhaustion caused by prolonged caregiving stress.

Is this therapy?
No. This platform provides general wellness support.

When should I seek professional help?
If stress or anxiety becomes persistent or overwhelming.
